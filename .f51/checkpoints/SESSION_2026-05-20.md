## ML Reward/Punishment System

Two components back the CEO's decisions:

1. **SDCA-Lite Cognitive Risk Firewall** (`src/core/risk_firewall/sdca_lite.py`)
   - 4 ghost token experts: MacroRisk, FlowRisk, HumanRisk, InfraRisk
   - Rumination cycles (up to 3) with uncertainty decay
   - Consequence scoring: delivery_7d, revenue_risk, chaos_reduction, truthfulness, cost_risk → utility score
   - Decision: ALLOW / REVIEW / BLOCK

2. **RewardFunction** (`src/core/trading/reward.py`)
   - PnL reward: proportional to profit, penalizes losses
   - Risk-adjusted: Sharpe-like normalized by risk taken
   - Confidence alignment: reward when confidence matches outcome
   - Duration penalty: penalizes holding too long without profit
   - Total reward clamped to [-1.0, 1.0]
